Following "informed" Polymarket wallets

Research for the Winner project · 20 Nov 2026 · 16,488 soccer games (Sep 2025 – Nov 18 2026) · 48.1 million trades with the wallet behind each one
Verdict: it doesn't work, so don't trade it. Some wallets really are skilled, and they stay skilled over time. But the price move they predict is tiny, about 0.4–0.6¢ a share. By the time a follower gets a fill, that move is already gone. Getting out before kickoff costs about 1.7¢ a share, and buying instantly adds about 1¢ more. We tried 4,110 rule combinations. None made money even on the data it was tuned on, and every family's best rule loses on the later periods.

1. What we tested

  • Data: every Polymarket trade on 16,488 soccer games, including the wallet behind it. We scored 363,892 wallets that took prices and 45,554 that placed resting orders.
  • Ways of spotting an "informed" wallet:
    • Its past trades predicted where the price went next. Scores were built month by month, each using only earlier games.
    • It often picks the winner.
    • It gets better prices than the price at kickoff.
    • A machine-learning model rates each bet.
  • "Insider-looking" behaviour: new wallets making big bets, several wallets betting together, bets that move the price a lot, bets placed 50–80 minutes before kickoff (when lineups come out), bets that are a big share of the game's volume, and wallets that only bet on one league.
  • Copying and betting against: for every pattern we tried both copying the bet and betting the opposite way.
  • Trading the way you asked:
    • We see the bet up to an hour later, like an hourly program would. We also tried 5, 15 and 60 minutes.
    • We buy either instantly, paying the fee, or with a limit order, which counts as filled only if a real trade went through our price.
    • We sell 1–5¢ higher, or at the price shortly before kickoff.
    • We never hold into the game.
  • Honest testing:
    • Rules were chosen on Sep 2025 – Jun 2026, checked on Jul–Sep 2026, then looked at once on Oct 1 – Nov 18 2026.
    • The 95% ranges come from resampling games.
    • We also checked that enough shares really traded at our price: about 60% of fills had $100 or more available, so order size is not the problem.

The costs are several times larger than anything a follower can capture. "Insider-looking" bets (new wallets, groups of wallets, bets that move the price a lot) are actually followed by the price moving back against them. Betting against them is still worth less than the cost of getting in and out.

5. What we can do next

  • Recommended: don't build the hourly trading agent now. No rule passed, and an AI agent can't create an edge that isn't in the data. Each hour it would only be picking among losing signals.
  • Optional: a cheap "watcher". An hourly program that paper-tracks the 3 least-bad rules on new games only (after 18 Nov), plus a control rule, with a site tab showing every signal and paper trade. It would start trading only if a rule shows a clearly positive result over 300+ paper trades and then holds up on the next 150. Expect it to confirm the losses. It costs a few dollars a month and needs no AI.
  • Hold-out note: the Oct–Nov period had already been used by earlier studies. The verdict doesn't depend on it, since everything loses on the training data too.
