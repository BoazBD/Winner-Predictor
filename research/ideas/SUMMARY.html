Six new Polymarket ideas: what works

Pre-kickoff soccer research · 21 Nov 2026 · each idea had its own research agent, real-trade fills, fees, and train / Jul–Sep / Oct–Nov testing
One idea works: catching panic sales (idea 2). We keep buy orders sitting about 20¢ below the price, and only when that is also at least 6¢ below Winner's fair price. They fill only when someone dumps into an empty order book, and the price usually bounces back within about 5 minutes. It returned +38% to +52% per $ in every period, but it's small: about 9 fills a month and roughly $190–400 a month. The other five ideas lose money or can't be captured.

  • My independent check:
    • Every fill has a real trade below our price at that moment.
    • Most of the profit comes from real sales at the target price, not from holding to the game result.
    • The price used to place each order is 2 minutes old, so there's no look-ahead.
  • Catches:
    • Fills are small (a median of about 25 shares), and a few big fills make most of the money.
    • Fills are getting rarer: about 50 a month in late 2025, about 10 a month in 2026.
    • Polymarket probably ties up the cost of every open order. That's why the "last 3 hours" version is the practical one.
    • 12–29% of fills end up held to the result because nobody buys back before kickoff.
  • Running it: an hourly program is enough, and no AI is needed. The rules are simple numbers, and Winner's fair price is the key filter.

Lessons from all the research so far

  • Polymarket's soccer prices before kickoff are about as accurate as Betfair's and Pinnacle's, and more accurate than Winner's. Ideas built on "the market is slow" (news, sharp books, other venues, informed wallets, buy-then-sell rules) all fail. The market isn't slow.
  • The two edges we found both come from other traders' impatience: people overpaying for longshots (the live Longshot seller) and people dumping into empty order books (catching panic sales).
  • An LLM agent adds nothing to the trading decisions. Where it might help is monitoring, such as weekly summaries and anomaly checks.
