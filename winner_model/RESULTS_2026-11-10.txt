WINNER BETTING MODEL - RESULTS (2026-11-10)
===========================================

Data: all_processed_winner_081126.parquet (52.5M rows, 321k markets) and
      processed_winner_081126.parquet (big leagues; subset of the first).
Bets: 1 unit the first time an outcome crosses the EV threshold, once per (market, outcome),
      only at prices actually present in that snapshot.

WHAT THE MODEL DOES (model.py)
------------------------------
1. Payout tiers. Winner prices 1X2 at exactly 90% / 85% / 80% payout by league
   (overround 1.111 / 1.176 / 1.25). Only the 90% tier is close enough to fair to matter.
2. Rounding. Prices are on a 0.05 grid; (overround - tier value) reveals whether the favourite's
   price was rounded up or down. Worth +-2-5% on short favourites.
3. Favourite-longshot correction. Odds-ratio de-margining + pooled logistic calibration on all
   122k soccer matches: 90%-tier favourites are about -4% to -6% before rounding.
4. Line moves. LightGBM predicts (calibrated closing value - current value). Works out of sample
   (predicted +4% -> realised +4.6%) but the big predicted moves are on outcomes that start
   around -22%; on favourites it adds 1-2%.
   EV = level EV at current price + predicted drift.

OTHER FINDINGS
--------------
- Flat ROI at close (dev period): soccer 1X2 -19..-24%, handicap -19..-25%, basketball spread -17%,
  tennis -19%. By odds, soccer 1X2: <=1.2 about -7%, 2-3 about -20%, >5 about -46%.
- Rounded-up favourites at odds <=1.10 (all tiers, close): +1.9% (n=103); rounded-down: -8 to -10%.
- Oracle: ~3% of minor-league 1X2 outcomes are at some point priced >3% above their calibrated
  closing value; betting those returns +10.9% +-2.2% over 3,615 bets. Median such price lasts 1 hour.
  Nothing in Winner's own data identifies them in advance: 1X2 and handicap markets move together
  82% of the time with no lead/lag, and the model never expects better than about -3% in volume.
- Margin formula: none of multiplicative / power / odds-ratio / additive / Shin fits exactly;
  odds-ratio is best calibrated in the mid range.

CONCLUSION
----------
Agree with the "feed + fixed margin" belief. Beating the margin needs the direction of the next
line move, i.e. timestamped sharp-bookmaker odds (Pinnacle / Betfair); lineup/news timing second.

NOT DONE
--------
- Basketball / baseball / American-football spreads dropped after baseline (-16..-20% flat, prices barely move).
- Tennis and soccer handicaps are scored but never came near the threshold.
- No neural sequence models. No subagents.
