{{ t.sweep }}: catching panic sales

A separate system from the models on the dashboard. In the last 3 hours before kickoff it keeps two cheap buy orders far below the price of every outcome, on both "Yes" and "No". They fill only when someone dumps into an empty order book, and the price usually bounces back within minutes. It then sells at the price from before the dump, or sells whatever is left 5 minutes before kickoff. Each order is 500 shares, and a fill counts only when a real Polymarket trade went below our price. It runs every 5 minutes. Simulation only, with no real money.

The backtest on 16k games (real trades, Sep 2025 – Nov 2026) expects about 5–7 fills a month, about +70% per $ spent on fills and about $150–220 a month at this size. Fills are rare and lumpy, so judge it over months, not days.

The rules
  • Which games: every Winner game matched to Polymarket, from 3 hours to 10 minutes before kickoff, for each outcome on both sides. Buying "Yes" catches dumps and buying "No" catches spikes. The price must be 8–92¢.
  • Two bids: level A at the lower of (price − 15¢) and 60% of the price; level B at the lower of (price − 20¢) and half the price. Each is 500 shares.
  • Winner check: we bid only if Winner's fair price for that side is at least 6¢ above our bid, so a fill means the dump pushed the price away from fair value.
  • Selling: a sell order at the price from before the dump, which counts only for shares real buyers bought above it. Whatever is left 5 minutes before kickoff is sold 1¢ under the last real sale (fee paid). If there was no such sale, it is held to the result.
  • Cooldown: 60 minutes after a fill before the same bid is placed again.
